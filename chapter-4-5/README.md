# HCA200 Study Arcade

A browser-based study arcade for HCA 200 Chapters 4, 5 and 10. Open `index.html` to play; no installation, account, or network connection is required.

## Included

- Eleven games covering ethics, client rights and consent, legal distinctions, worker protections, teamwork, supervision, assigning and delegation.
- 97 concept and scenario questions, including 43 Chapter 10 prompts weighted toward lecture quiz-focus topics with shuffled answer choices and immediate explanations.
- Round scoring, chapter/topic filters, saved local progress, and a retry queue for missed questions.
- Responsive layout and keyboard shortcuts 1–4 to choose an answer.

## Sources

Chapter 10 emphasis is informed by Therese’s lecture consolidation notes and checked against the supplied Chapter 10 text. The notes identify the Five Rights of Delegation, accepting/refusing delegation, decision factors, role limits, conflict resolution, care conferences and supervision as quiz priorities; the arcade marks the highest-priority Chapter 10 games.


Built from the supplied Chapter 4 Ethics PowerPoint and full Chapter 4 text; the Chapter 5 Legislation: The Client’s Rights and Your Rights PowerPoint and full Chapter 5 text; and the Chapter 10 Complete Quiz Study Guide and Working With Others: Teamwork, Supervision, and Delegation PowerPoint and full Chapter 10 text. Slide decks guide topic emphasis; chapter texts add definitions, examples and scenario depth. Each answer displays its source note.

Delegation guidelines and workplace policies can vary by province, territory and setting. The arcade uses the course materials’ terminology and directs policy-specific or out-of-scope questions to a supervisor or regulated health-care professional.
