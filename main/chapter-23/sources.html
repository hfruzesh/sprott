Source guide

This independent practice quiz uses three user-supplied sources. References are shown with each answer.

  • Chapter 23: Social media or “not present in person” communication and the healthcare professional — supplied reading extract. Section titles identify the relevant discussion.
  • HCA200 Day 5 PowerPoint — Social Media, slides 3–13. Slide 12 supplies all ten NETIQUETTE terms.
  • HCA200 Chapter 23 Quiz Preparation Notes, pages 1–4. Section numbers identify topics, including observer responses and simplified libel/slander distinctions.

The questions are original practice items, not an official assessment. Historical usage statistics are not presented as current facts. The final E in NETIQUETTE is Empathic. Legal terms follow the simplified study distinctions and do not determine the legal status of a particular case.

There are 100 questions across three difficulty levels, with shuffled answer choices, explanations, saved progress and a retry queue. The mixed quiz draws up to 20 questions; the full quiz includes all 100.
