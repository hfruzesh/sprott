# HCA Study Arcade — answer-key audit

Source: HCAPLP-PowerPoint - HCA Pre-Learning Package  (1).pptx, 47 slides. Scope: all 155 questions, their four choices, explanations, distractor feedback, and the derived 155 flashcards. The original PowerPoint slide XML was read directly and compared with the current question bank. This is an audit of agreement with the supplied teaching deck, not independent verification of its clinical or statistical claims.

## Findings and corrections

- All 155 keyed answers are supported by the deck, with the scope clarifications below. The lymphatic answer now explicitly says lymphatic vessels.
- Item 7: narrowed the prompt to cognitive/mental health needs so the other client groups do not create ambiguity.
- Item 52: specified the general intestine term; explained that col/o refers specifically to the colon, which is part of the intestine.
- Item 97: replaced the vague word vessels with lymphatic vessels, matching slide 22.
- Item 153: corrected feedback to say the Book Club activity is handed in WITH the pre-clinical self-assessment before clinical placement, matching slide 46.
- Item 3: the source is internally inconsistent. Slide 3 requires checklist completion before the program begins; slides 41–43 place some checklist tasks in the first week. The question now explicitly asks about the introductory requirement, and the explanation discloses the conflict and advises clarification from the campus/instructor.
- Slide-reported estimates and dated statements remain attributed to the deck. No outside factual corrections were introduced. These include slides 8, 23, 24, and the WorkSafeBC (2013) statement on slide 25.
- The PPE abbreviation uses personal protective equipment as expressly defined on slide 20. Slide 9 uses the heading Personal Protection Equipment; that heading variation does not change the keyed expansion from slide 20.
- The myalgia explanation uses my/o, consistent with slide 15; slide 16 contains a root-word typo (Mya). The keyed meaning muscle pain agrees with slide 16.

## Grading and consistency checks

The bank evaluates successfully as JavaScript. All 155 IDs are unique and preserved. Each question has four distinct options; the keyed answer occurs once; all three distractor explanations match their respective choice. Flashcard answers match quiz keys. Practice and exam grading compare the selected answer text with the keyed answer, so shuffling answer order does not change the correct answer. The 45-question exam draws from this same audited bank.
